Features

Everything you'd note down after each match, done for you

Matches and games

Each Bo3 is one row with its score (2-1, 1-2…) that opens to show every game: play or draw, result and mulligans.

Hands and screenshots

Your opening hand, the hand you kept and screenshots of the key moments. Press F8 or the button over the game to save any moment you want and add a note to it. Screenshots are saved on your PC and can be turned off in Settings.

Opponent's deck

It compares the opponent's cards with the most played decks of the game's format and names the closest one. The meta lists update every 3 days.

Statistics

Match and game win rates, streaks, last days, and breakdowns by mulligans, play/draw, deck and opponent archetype.

Your decks

Each list you play with its win rate. Sideboard variants are grouped under the same deck, and you can rename them. In Magic Online, a deck takes the name you saved it with when the list matches exactly; otherwise it is named by its colors.

AI loss review (subscribers)

Subscribers get an AI analysis of every lost game: what went wrong, where you could have played better and what to try next. Subscription: $6 per month on Metafy.

How it works

From the Arena and Magic Online logs to your history

FAQ

Before you start

Does it touch the game or give me an advantage in a match?

No, neither in Arena nor in Magic Online. It only reads the files the games write on your PC (Arena's Player.log; Magic Online's mtgo.log, Match_GameLog_*.dat, mtgo_game_history and grouping *.xml deck files), without modifying them. It doesn't modify Arena or Magic Online, doesn't read their memory and doesn't connect to their servers. The optional overlay is a separate window that only shows public information or what can be worked out from what has been played, never hidden information.

What data does it send?

Your games, screenshots and statistics stay in a folder on your PC. The program downloads the updated meta deck lists, one per format (every 3 days), looks up card names on Scryfall (also with Magic Online ids) when it needs them, and asks GitHub once a day whether there's a new version. You can turn those three off in Settings. It also downloads a small content file from manalab.es (when it opens and every 6 hours) and shows a Metafy ad at the end of the Dashboard, which you can remove with the X or in Settings › Data and about ManaLab. The Learn section asks a ManaLab server for the list of guides and loads covers from Metafy and photos from manalab.es, sending nothing of yours except the ManaLab version; the Metafy banner loads Metafy's ad script and turns off in Settings. Missing card images are downloaded from Scryfall. It sends data out only when you ask for something. The AI analysis sends that game's data (result, deck, hands, cards seen and timeline), never your name or the opponent's. A bug report sends what you write, your email if you add one, the ManaLab version, your system and language, whether you use Arena or Magic Online, an anonymous ID of your PC and, if you tick it, the last lines of registro.log with your user name and codes removed. It sends no games or decks.

More details: Is ManaLab safe?

Windows says «Windows protected your PC». Is it safe?

That warning appears for programs without a paid code-signing certificate, which is common for small free tools. Click «More info» and then «Run anyway». It only shows on the first download: new versions install with «Update now» from inside the app, with no warning. To avoid it the first time too, before unzipping right-click the ZIP › Properties › tick «Unblock» › OK. Every version is checked on VirusTotal before it's published.

More details: Is ManaLab safe?

Does it work with Magic Online?

Yes, from 2.3. ManaLab records your Magic Online (MTGO) games: hands, mulligans, play or draw, the opponent's deck, statistics, replay and overlay. There is nothing to set up: it starts by itself when you open Magic Online, and you can import an mtgo.log file. What is different: Magic Online doesn't send turn phases or tapped cards, so ManaLab works them out from the log, and in the replay anything worked out is marked ≈ because it may not be exact. Magic Online doesn't give your sideboarded list in time either: ManaLab asks you to confirm your changes between games with one click, and until you do the overlay shows the list as unconfirmed. The overlay can show less than in Arena when the log has no table state. Your deck takes the name you saved it with in Magic Online when the list matches exactly; otherwise it is named by its colors.

On the website: Magic Online tracker: what it saves and how it works

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

For now it's for Windows 10 and 11. There is no native Linux version, and it has not been tested with Wine or Proton, so we can't promise it will work.

Does it log Draft and Sealed (Limited)?

Yes. It logs any Arena or Magic Online game, including Limited. The opponent's deck is compared with the meta decks of the game's format (Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage and Pauper); if nothing clearly matches, as in Limited, it is named by its colors.

Is it free?

The program is free and needs no account. The only paid part is the AI loss analysis: $6 per month, which pays for the AI service it uses. Every other feature is free.

How is ManaLab free?

The tracker is free and always will be. The only paid part is the optional AI analysis of your losses, a membership on Metafy. In the Learn section you'll also find guides from Metafy.
