Giveaway rules

Translation of the Spanish rules above. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

  1. Organizer. The giveaway is organized by the developer of the ManaLab application, an individual resident in Spain (hereinafter, the "Organizer"). Contact: manalab.app@gmail.com. Website: https://manalab.es
  2. Purpose and scope. The giveaway promotes the launch of ManaLab 2.2 through the X account @ManaLabApp. It is free: no purchase, payment or economic consideration of any kind is required.
  3. Period. Entries are accepted from the publication of the giveaway post, Monday 26 October 2026, until Monday 2 November 2026 at 23:59 (Madrid time). The draw will take place on Tuesday 3 November 2026.
  4. How to enter. To enter, during the period indicated, participants must complete the following three actions: a) follow the @ManaLabApp account; b) repost (retweet) the giveaway post; and c) reply to the giveaway post describing the worst keep they have had this week in MTG Arena. The content of the reply is not judged: replying is enough. Each person may enter only once.
  5. Eligibility. Participants must be over 18 years old and have a public, genuine X account. Fake accounts, multiple accounts or accounts created to enter the giveaway are excluded, as are accounts belonging to the Organizer and to people in their circle. The giveaway is valid worldwide, except where prohibited by local law.
  6. Prize. There will be 3 prizes. Each winner receives a 1-month subscription code for ManaLab's AI loss analysis, with a value of €5 (€15 in total). The prize cannot be exchanged for money and is not transferable, and it can only be used in the ManaLab application, which runs on Windows.
  7. Selection of winners. Winners will be chosen at random from all valid entries, using a random draw tool (for example, random.org). 3 alternates will also be selected using the same procedure. The Organizer will publish how the draw was carried out.
  8. Announcement and delivery. Winners will be announced by replying to the giveaway post on Tuesday 3 November 2026. The Organizer will contact each winner by X direct message (DM). The winner will have seven days from that contact to respond; if they do not, the prize will pass to the corresponding alternate. The code will be sent by DM or by email, as the winner prefers.
  9. Disqualification. The Organizer may disqualify any entry that breaches these rules, is fraudulent, or has been obtained through fake accounts or any other manipulation.
  10. Changes and cancellation. The Organizer may modify or cancel the giveaway for a justified reason, announcing this on X.
  11. Relationship with X and Wizards of the Coast. X (Twitter) does not sponsor, endorse or administer this giveaway, and is in no way associated with it. Wizards of the Coast is not associated with it either. "ManaLab is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards."
  12. Data protection. Data controller: the Organizer. Only the X username of participants is used to manage the giveaway; for winners, the delivery channel they choose (DM or email) is also used. Legal basis: performance of these rules (Art. 6.1(b) GDPR). Data is not shared with third parties and is deleted once the prizes have been delivered. You may exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure by writing to manalab.app@gmail.com, and you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
  13. Taxes. The value of each prize (€5) does not exceed €300, so no withholding or payment on account will be applied.
  14. Acceptance. Entering the giveaway means accepting these rules in full.
  15. Applicable law. These rules are governed by Spanish law. Any dispute will be subject to the courts that have jurisdiction under the applicable regulations.
