What it does

More of the lesson on decisions, less on prep

ManaLab is a free tracker for MTG Arena and Magic Online. It saves opening hands, mulligans, play or draw, the opponent's deck and a replay of every game on its own.

Less prep

Your student picks the games and sends you one report.

Visible decisions

You see the hand, the mulligan and the key moments of each game.

Repeated mistakes

Tags show what keeps going wrong and how it changes over 30 or 90 days.

Sideboarding with data

Your student keeps a plan per matchup, and you see the changes they really made.

The app is free and needs no account. Your student needs it to create any of these files; you only need to install it for Team mode or to open exported games.

Before the lesson

Send these steps to your student

It takes a few minutes if they already have saved games.

  1. Install ManaLab from manalab.es and keep it open while playing.
  2. Play as usual. To flag a doubtful decision, press F8 (the default key): it saves a screenshot of that moment.
  3. Tag mistakes. In each game's Notes tab, add tags and write down any questions.
  4. Choose what to review. In Games, open «⋯» › «Prepare session» and tick the games.
  5. Create and send the report. Press «Create session report», save it as PDF if you like, and send it to the coach.

The report includes hands, mulligans, key moments, a turn summary, notes and highlights. It works offline, and your student can hide their opponents' names.

For the first lesson, also ask for «Export for my coach», in the «⋯» menu of Statistics. It gives you their summary, matchups and tagged mistakes.

Message to send your student

Before our lesson, install ManaLab (manalab.es). It's free and needs no account. Keep it open while you play and press F8 on any play you're unsure about. Then, in Games, open ⋯ › Prepare session, choose 3 games and send me the report.

During the lesson

Start with what repeats

Then move to specific games. This order works well for a one-hour lesson:

  1. Trends. Open «What repeats the most» in Statistics and choose one theme to work on.
  2. Matchups. The table separates game 1 from post-sideboard games. An asterisk means fewer than 10 games, so don't draw firm conclusions from those rows.
  3. Hands and mulligans. Review these before the plays: was it a keep?
  4. Replay. Jump to the key moments and discuss the decisions tied to your theme.
  5. Sideboard. Compare their plan with what they really brought in and took out.

With Team mode you see each game in the same detail as your student: hands, screenshots with notes (if they share them), replay and the opponent's deck.

After the lesson

Leave one or two goals, not a long list

The next lesson can then start by checking whether they improved.

  • Tags: always use the same names, so «What repeats the most» tracks progress properly.
  • Notes: in Team mode, write your note in the game's Notes tab.
  • Follow-up: next lesson, check that mistake over 30 days before opening new games.

How to turn on Team mode

  1. Create a shared folder in Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive.
  2. Each of you opens Settings › Team and AI and sets the folder, your name and your role: player or coach.
  3. Press «Sync now».

Each person writes only in their own subfolder. By default ManaLab publishes all of your student's games to the shared folder; they can hide opponents' names, turn off screenshot sharing or stop sharing altogether.

How your note reaches your student

Open the Team screen, choose your student and a game, and write in the box «Your coach note for this game…» of its Notes tab. The note is saved to the shared folder when you leave the box; you don't need «Sync now» for it. Your student sees it in that game's Notes tab once their folder service has synced the file and they open the tab.

Arena and Magic Online

What to keep in mind for Magic Online

Reviewing a student works with both games. For Magic Online, keep four things in mind:

  • Sideboard: your student must confirm their changes between games with one click («Use this change», «No changes» or «Edit»). Games never confirmed don't count in the sideboard changes shown under Sideboard.
  • Replay: phases and tapped cards that aren't confirmed are marked ≈, because they may not be exact.
  • Deck names: a Magic Online deck is usually named by its colors, so ask your student what it is.
  • Imports: importing a log doesn't correct the sideboard of games already saved.

In Arena, turn on Options › Account › “Detailed Logs (Plugin Support)” once and restart the game. Magic Online needs no setting.

FAQ

Before you start

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. The only paid part is the AI loss analysis, which you don't need for anything in this guide.

Does my student share their whole history?

With a report or a .manalab file they share only what they choose. In Team mode, ManaLab publishes all their games to your shared folder unless they stop sharing; they can hide opponents' names and turn off screenshot sharing.

Do their games count in my statistics?

No. Games from another player stay marked and don't count in yours.

What does an imported game leave out?

Screenshots and AI analysis. It keeps the replay, hands, mulligans, deck, notes and tags.

What do I need?

ManaLab runs on Windows 10 and 11. Reports open in any browser, so you don't need to install anything to read them.

What if something's missing?

Write to manalab.app@gmail.com. The coaching tools are being built around what you ask for.
