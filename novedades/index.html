New in 2.3

Now for Arena and Magic Online

Version 2.3 brings Magic Online to ManaLab, with hands, statistics, replays and overlay, and adds a few things for everyone.

Magic Online, logged on its own

MTGO games are recorded with their hands, mulligans, play or draw and the opponent's deck, and the overlay works there too. Your deck is named by its colors.

Everything ManaLab does for Magic Online →

Lands list in the overlay

Hover over «Lands» in the overlay to open every land in your deck with its copies left and the chance of drawing it next. You can turn it off in Settings › Overlay › My panel.

Report a bug

A «Report a bug» button inside the app lets you tell us what went wrong, with the latest lines of the log if you choose to attach them.

Statistics by real format

Each game counts in its real format, and you can filter the statistics by format.

Redesigned interface

The whole app has a new look.

User manual in English

A PDF manual that comes with every version.

Download the manual (PDF) →

Dashboard

Arena and Magic Online, side by side

The Dashboard shows the state of each game and your latest matches, with a small MTGO tag on the ones you played in Magic Online.

Sideboard

Confirm your sideboard in one click

Magic Online doesn't send your sideboarded list in time, so between games a small panel asks whether you changed it: use last time's change, say there were none, or open the editor.

Magic Online in ManaLab: what is the same and what is different

  • Nothing to set up. ManaLab starts recording by itself when you open Magic Online. You can also import an mtgo.log file to bring in games you played with ManaLab closed.
  • Your main tools, in Magic Online. Hands, mulligans, play or draw, the opponent's deck, statistics, replay and overlay.
Earlier · Version 2.2

Study your games and work with your coach

Version 2.2 was about reviewing: replays, reports and trends.

Replay

Every game, replayed with the real cards

A board with the real card images, key moments and your coach's comments pinned to each step. In Magic Online, phases and tapped cards are deduced and marked ≈.

Session report

A session report for your coach

Press «Prepare session» and pick the games: you get one file with hands, key moments and notes, to review or send to your coach.

Tags and trends

Coach tags and trends

Your coach tags mistakes per game or per step, and «What repeats the most» shows how each one evolves over 30 or 90 days, or your whole history.

Overlay

A redesigned overlay

New look, card names in your language and the chance of drawing each card in your next 1, 2 or 3 draws.
